Industries & Applications

Six application areas where DICE’s listed capabilities may support a project requirement — and the capability mix each one typically draws on.

How to read this page

Application areas, not a client list

The sections below describe the kinds of work the listed DICE capabilities can support. They are illustrative application areas, not verified customer projects, and the list is not exhaustive.

No customer names, project histories, sector accreditations or industry endorsements are stated anywhere on this page. If your requirement does not obviously match a section below, it is still worth describing — the technical review comes before any routing decision.

Application area 01

Application area 01

Process equipment and industrial assemblies

Equipment and assemblies that sit inside a process line, where the item has to suit the duty, the medium and the standard it is built to.

Work of this kind usually crosses several capabilities: drawings have to be developed or interpreted, plate and sections have to be cut and formed, joints have to be welded by qualified personnel, and inspection records have to follow the item through to handover.

Application area 02

Application area 02

Pipe spools, bends and piping-related components

Spools, bends and the fittings around them, where fit-up at site depends entirely on dimensional control in the shop.

Bend method matters here. Induction and cold forming suit different combinations of radius, material and wall thickness, and the choice is made during technical review rather than assumed. End preparation, marking and spool identification are what make site assembly straightforward.

Application area 03

Application area 03

Structural steel and fabricated components

Frames, supports, platforms and the brackets and fixtures that hold everything else in place.

Structural work is unforgiving about cumulative error, so connection detailing and hole position tend to decide whether erection goes smoothly. Engineering review before fabrication is usually the cheapest part of the whole scope.

Application area 04

Application area 04

Precision-machined components

Close-tolerance parts, single pieces or repeat batches, where tolerance and finish drive the manufacturing route.

Material, tolerance, surface finish and quantity together decide which equipment genuinely suits a part. Confirming those four things properly at enquiry stage is what prevents a component being made on the wrong machine.

Application area 05

Application area 05

Industrial installation and specialist execution

Work where the activity happens on site rather than in a shop, and where access and sequence shape what is possible.

Site scope brings constraints that have nothing to do with the drawing: permits, access windows, working at height, and other trades in the same space. Those are raised at review stage, because they often change the method entirely.

Application area 06

Engineering design and manufacturing documentation

The design and documentation layer that every other application area depends on.

Concept development, 3D modelling, manufacturing drawings, jig and fixture design and analysis where it is in scope. Revision control and a clear issue status matter as much as the geometry, because everyone downstream works from whatever was released.
