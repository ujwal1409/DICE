Who we are

A single engineering interface for complex requirements

DICE Engineering & Consultancy coordinates engineering expertise, specialist partners, manufacturing capabilities and execution support through one customer interface.

Requirements rarely arrive as finished specifications. They arrive as drawings that need review, components that need a suitable process route, assemblies that need fabrication, or site activities that need skilled people. DICE takes that requirement, reviews it technically, and then selects the capability best suited to it.

The appropriate capability is selected according to the customer's specific requirement — scope, material, tolerance, volume, standard and timeline. Depending on the work, that may mean in-house engineering coordination, a specialist partner facility, a manufacturing route, or deployed manpower.

  • One point of contact for enquiry, technical review and follow-up.
  • Capability matched to the requirement rather than forced into a fixed offering.
  • Engineering documentation and coordination across every stage.
About DICE See how a requirement is handled

DICE operates as a coordinating engineering and consultancy interface. Services are delivered through a combination of in-house coordination and selected specialist partners. DICE does not claim to own every facility or to perform every listed process in-house.

How DICE works

From requirement to delivery in five steps

A consistent route for every enquiry, so scope, technical intent and responsibility stay clear from first contact to handover.

  1. 1

    Requirement

    You share drawings, specifications, quantities and timeline — or simply describe the problem.

  2. 2

    Technical Review

    Scope, materials, tolerances, standards and constraints are reviewed by engineering.

  3. 3

    Partner Match

    The suitable capability or specialist facility is identified for the specific requirement.

  4. 4

    Execution

    Work proceeds under coordinated progress tracking and agreed inspection points.

  5. 5

    Delivery

    Documentation, dispatch and handover are completed through the same single interface.

Partner network

Capability coordinated to suit the project
