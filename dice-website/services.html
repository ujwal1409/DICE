Engineering & CAD

Category 01

Engineering & CAD

Design and documentation that production can actually build from — from first concept through to issued manufacturing drawings.

Technical review happens here first. Scope, materials, tolerances and standards are assessed, then the design intent is carried into models and drawings that a workshop can work to without guessing.

Capabilities

  • 2D drawing and drafting
  • Conceptual design and 3D modelling
  • Manufacturing drawings
  • 3D animation
  • FEA design analysis
  • Jig and fixture design

Software capabilities

  • AutoCAD
  • Autodesk Inventor
  • Siemens NX
  • SolidWorks
Request a Quote

Typical inputs

  • Sketches, concepts or a written brief
  • Existing drawings and revision history
  • Samples or measured dimensions
  • Applicable specifications and standards

Typical outputs

  • General arrangement and detail drawings
  • 3D models and assemblies
  • Bills of material
  • Fabrication and manufacturing drawings
  • Analysis summaries and animation files

Good to know

  • Drawing standard, units and projection are confirmed before work starts
  • Revision control is maintained throughout
  • FEA scope, load cases and assumptions are agreed before analysis
  • Software listed above describes available capability, not vendor certification
Fabrication

Category 02

Fabrication

Fabricated assemblies, pipe spools, bends and structural components produced to drawing and specification.

Fabrication work is routed to the capability that matches the material, size, welding requirement and inspection standard involved, with documentation collected as the work proceeds.

Capabilities

  • Process equipment and fabricated assemblies
  • Pipe spools and bends
  • Structural and process equipment
  • Fixtures and components
  • Relevant non-destructive examination (NDE) scope, where applicable
Request a Quote

Typical inputs

  • Fabrication and spool drawings
  • Material specification and grade
  • Welding and joint requirements
  • Inspection and test plan, where applicable

Typical outputs

  • Fabricated assemblies and sub-assemblies
  • Pipe spools and formed bends
  • Structural members and fixtures
  • Weld and material records
  • NDE reports where NDE is in scope

Good to know

  • NDE method and extent are confirmed per project, not assumed
  • Material traceability is maintained where the specification requires it
  • Welding procedures and qualification follow the applicable standard for the work
Precision Manufacturing

Category 03

Precision Manufacturing

Close-tolerance machining and cutting, from single components through to repeat batches.

Tolerance, surface finish, material and quantity decide the route. Those are confirmed during technical review so the component is made on equipment genuinely suited to it.

Capabilities

  • Precision CNC machining
  • CNC milling and component processing
  • Waterjet cutting
  • Heavy-thickness cutting
  • Specialist machining
Request a Quote

Typical inputs

  • Machining drawings or 3D models
  • Tolerance and surface finish requirements
  • Material grade and condition
  • Quantity and delivery requirement

Typical outputs

  • Machined components and assemblies
  • Cut profiles, blanks and plate parts
  • Dimensional inspection records

Good to know

  • Achievable tolerance is confirmed before routing rather than promised in advance
  • Waterjet cutting suits heat-sensitive materials as it introduces no heat-affected zone
  • Heavy-thickness capability depends on the selected facility and is confirmed per enquiry
Specialist Execution

Category 04

Specialist Execution

The processes that turn drawings and cut material into finished, installed work.

Welding, forming, cutting, assembly and installation support carried out by suitably qualified personnel, with the method selected to suit the material, geometry and standard.

Capabilities

  • Welding
  • Assembly and installation
  • Laser and plasma cutting
  • Pipe cutting and preparation
  • Induction and cold bends
  • Project-specific installation support
Request a Quote

Typical inputs

  • Drawings, weld maps and joint details
  • Applicable procedures and standards
  • Site, access and interface information
  • Programme and sequence requirements

Typical outputs

  • Welded joints and completed assemblies
  • Cut and prepared pipe ends
  • Induction and cold formed bends
  • Installation and fit-up support records

Good to know

  • Welder qualification is matched to the applicable standard for the joint
  • Bending method — induction or cold — is selected by radius, material and wall thickness
  • Site activity is subject to access, permit and safety requirements at the location

Skilled personnel provided for defined project needs, matched to the trade and qualification the work actually requires.

Where a project needs additional hands rather than a manufactured item, DICE coordinates trades and technical personnel for the required discipline, duration and location.

Capabilities

  • Welders
  • Fitters
  • Designers
  • NDE personnel
  • Skilled trade workers
  • Project-specific and overseas manpower support

Typical inputs

Typical outputs

  • Qualification and documentation records

How capability is selected

The category is the starting point, not the answer

Listing six categories keeps the catalogue readable, but real requirements usually cross them. A single enquiry might need drafting, plate profiling, machining, welding and two trades on site. That is normal, and it is the reason DICE reviews the requirement before proposing a route.

Capability is coordinated through DICE and selected specialist partners according to project requirements, scope, availability and agreed arrangements.
